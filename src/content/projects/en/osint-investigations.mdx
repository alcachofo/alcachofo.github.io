Each investigation shows the process rather than just the conclusion: what the initial lure looked like, which signals gave it away, which public records were pulled, and where the trail went cold. They are written so someone with no background can follow the reasoning and repeat the steps.

Everything uses publicly available information only, and findings are reported to the relevant authorities and abuse contacts before or alongside publication.

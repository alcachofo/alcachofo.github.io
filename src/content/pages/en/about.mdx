## Who am I

I'm **Alcachofo**, a CTF player and security researcher from Spain. I play mostly web, but I'll happily dig into reversing, crypto, pwn, forensics, OSINT and web3 when a CTF throws them at me.

The name is Spanish for artichoke. It fits: every challenge is layers you peel one by one until you reach the heart, which here is the flag.

## Results

- **1st of 500** in the ECSC 2026 Spanish Qualifier (INCIBE), playing solo
- **5th of 129** in the Cátedra Ada Byron UAH CTF (INCIBE), with Grey Company

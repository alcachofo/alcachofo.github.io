**Category:** OSINT

**Author:** Ask the frog

## Challenge

Every onboarding presentation at Brunnerne Inc.™ includes the same grainy old photo: a small house that management proudly describes as "the original home bakery that started it all". Some even claim it is the very birthplace of the brunsviger - although Legal insists we clarify that this remains disputed.

Management wanted a fresh photo for this year's slides, so the previous intern was sent to find and document the historic site and returned with the attached image. The location should be right, but apparently a renovated modern house does not communicate the humble beginnings and artisanal values, management wanted.

As the newest intern at Brunnerne Inc.™, you have been assigned to the Historical Pastry Assets department to take over the previous intern's tasks... and position. Maybe a deep dive into the history of the brunsviger is in order.

Find where the photo was taken from, and identify the building's original name.

Flag format: brunner\{\<what3words>_\<name-of-building>\} where \<what3words> is the what3words address of the photographer's location (with 3 word address language set to English).
Example: brunner\{cried.desktop.drip_odense-banegaard\}.

Note: we accept multiple what3words locations within a 6x12 m^2 rectangle of the true position.


Then you are given a jpg called birthplace.jpg

## Solution

### Finding brunsviger origin: 

After a deep investigation, we found that the origin story was related to "Brylle Sogn" near Tommerup there was a settlement called "Brunsvig", existed.

### Finding the house

I drove Tobovej in Street View and found it after a while. 

The address is "Tobovej 68, 5690 Tommerup, matr. 6a Gundestrup By, Brylle."

### Using what3words 

Simple, i first put it in english, and then I selected the square that corresponded to the the image closest to the adddress and it worked. I got wasp.shortfalls.triathlon

### The building's name

After a while of looking at articles, I found: https://fyens.dk/bagsiden/brunsvigersafari-3-det-skjulte-fynske-brunsvig

where i could find: "Lille Brunsvig" as the name

### Flag

combining the what3words result and the building name applying the format, we get:
```
brunner{wasp.shortfalls.triathlon_lille-brunsvig}
```
